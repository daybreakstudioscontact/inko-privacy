Privacy Policy

Inko — Draw, Paint & Sketch

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Inko is a drawing app made by Daybreak Studios. Inko is built to work entirely on your own device. It does not have accounts, it does not show ads, and it does not send your drawings or any personal information anywhere. This policy explains that in plain terms.

The short version

  • Inko collects no personal data.
  • Your drawings, palettes, brushes and backups stay on your device.
  • Inko makes no network connections and has no internet permission.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

What Inko stores, and where

Everything you make in Inko — your drawings and their earlier versions, your saved palettes, your custom brushes, and any backup file you create — is stored locally on your device using the browser storage built into Android (IndexedDB). None of it is uploaded or shared. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this content is removed from your device, so we recommend using Inko’s built-in backup if you want to keep it.

Camera

Inko can use your device’s camera for one optional purpose: to sample colours from what the camera sees, or to take a photo to use as a drawing reference. The camera is only ever used after you choose a camera feature and Android asks your permission. Images from the camera are processed on your device and are never uploaded or stored by us. You can use the whole app without ever granting camera access.

Permissions Inko requests

  • Camera — optional, only for the colour-from-photo and photo reference features described above.

Inko does not request internet, location, contacts, microphone, storage of your files, or any other sensitive permission.

Data sharing and third parties

Inko contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking libraries, and does not share any data with anyone, because no data ever leaves your device.

Children

Inko is safe for all ages. Because it collects no data and has no network features, there is no personal information to collect from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same web address with a new “Last updated” date.
